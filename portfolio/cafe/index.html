Our Story

Started in a home kitchen. Still run like one.

Ember & Crumb began in 2016 as a Saturday farmers-market stall with one folding table and a sourdough starter named Geraldine. She's still with us — she's in every loaf we sell.

In 2019 we took over this corner storefront, kept the original tin ceiling, and built the bakery around a single deck oven. No freezers full of shipped-in dough, no shortcuts: flour, water, salt, time.

Today we're a staff of nine, a wall of regulars' mugs, and the unofficial meeting room of the neighborhood. Come for a loaf; stay because someone saved you the window seat.
