Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

How long does it take?

About two weeks from our first conversation to a live site, for a standard small-business site. If you need it faster for a season opening or an event, say so — we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

What do you need from me?

Your business details (services, prices, hours, address, phone), any photos you want used, and your logo if you have one — if you don't, clean text-based branding is included. That's it. We write the page copy with you, so you're never staring at a blank document.

I already have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

A Facebook page is a good companion, but it can't be your whole presence: people without Facebook accounts hit walls, Google sends most "near me" searches to real websites, and you don't control how the page looks or whether the platform changes the rules. Your own site is the one piece of the internet you actually own. We'll link the two together so they reinforce each other.

Do I own my website?

Yes — the site and the domain are yours. If you ever move to another designer or bring it in-house, everything goes with you. The care plan is a service, not a lock-in.

What does the $30/month care plan actually cover?

Hosting, keeping the site running and secure, and small content changes — updated prices, seasonal hours, a new menu, fresh photos. Bigger additions (a whole new page, an online store) are quoted separately, always before any work starts.

What if I want changes after the 30 days?

Care plan members just ask. Everyone else can request changes at a fair, quoted-up-front price — most small edits are quick and inexpensive, and we'll always tell you the cost before doing the work.

Ready to be findable?

Send us a note with your business name and what you do. We'll reply with honest thoughts on what your site should include — and if we're not the right fit, we'll say that too.
